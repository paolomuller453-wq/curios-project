# Facilitator run sheet — Curios of Nancy

**Group size:** 6 to 40 people in 2–6 teams of 3–7.
**Duration:** 15 / 25 / 35 minutes of play, plus 10 minutes of debrief.
**Equipment:** one tablet or phone (or a laptop on a projector, with the keyboard as buzzers). Sound on is better.

## Before (5 min)
1. Open the game link and tap **New expedition**.
2. Form teams, mixing people who don't usually work together. Let each team **choose its name and mascot**: it's the first team decision.
3. Choose the length and tap **Start the expedition!**
4. Read the rules aloud, or show them with **How to play** on the title screen.

## During
- **Pass the device.** On team challenges, the active team holds the tablet. On "All teams" challenges, put it in the middle of the table.
- **Buzzers:** on a laptop or projector, keys 1–6 are the team buzzers.
- **Steals keep everyone listening.** When a team misses a quiz question, every other team can steal it.
- **Macaron Toss:** make sure a *different* teammate throws each time.
- **Perks** (Mira's Help, Double Down) are earned by levelling up. Encourage teams to discuss when to use them.
- **The Golden Gates** is a shared goal. Remind teams that every point helps everyone, not just their own team.
- **The ☰ menu** lets you add or remove points (e.g. a bonus for the best team cheer), skip a challenge, or jump to the finale if time runs short.

## Debrief (10 min)
The finale shows the podium, an **award for every team**, the Golden Gates result, and five debrief questions:
1. What did your team do well together today?
2. Who took the lead on each challenge — and did that change?
3. What surprised you most about Nancy's heritage?
4. Which challenge needed the most listening? Why?
5. If you visited one of today's places for real, which would it be?

**Tip:** end with a real tasting — Nancy macarons, bergamotes, a slice of quiche lorraine or mirabelle tart — or a walk to one of the places the teams discovered.
